# Bailey Nelson leasing intelligence — screen-share guide

## Purpose

Use the tool as a public-data decision screen: find a potential market, understand why it ranks, inspect exact-place optical competition and public property relationships, save the place, then export a brief for follow-up. Scores narrow the field; they do not predict store success.

## Current census position

As of 3 September 2026 the working census contains:

- 2,151 observed optical store records across Australia and New Zealand.
- 1,503 canonical retail places: 691 shopping centres and 812 high-street corridors.
- Complete location-setting and place mapping for 1,550 named-network stores.
- 6,192 unpromoted map leads retained as background discovery evidence rather than manual-review failures.

Counts are generated from reconciled sources. They are not hard-coded targets. Independent coverage remains less complete than named-network coverage.

## Quick orientation before presenting

Open the question-mark icon in the app header for the built-in five-minute tour. The map toolbar works as follows:

- **Layers** changes contextual overlays; it does not change the underlying store census.
- **Store distance** lets you select two existing stores and draws their straight-line separation.
- **Candidate** turns the next blank-map click into a hypothetical site. That point inherits its containing public demographic area and nearby network evidence.
- **Save view** stores the current public view, filters, map position and candidate points in that browser and restores them on a later base-page visit. It does not create a shareable link.
- **Share** copies a sanitised URL. Local corrections, place shortlist entries and private performance data are excluded.
- **Brief** prints the currently open place or selected candidate. Open the intended record first.
- **Export** is context-aware: Network exports selected stores, Places exports filtered places, Opportunity exports the current Bailey-free place results and Compare exports candidate evidence.

Normal marker clicks remain inspection actions: a store opens its store detail and nearby network; a place opens exact-place tenants and nearby competition. Candidate mode is deliberately separate so inspecting an existing marker does not accidentally create a candidate.

The optional **Load private Bailey benchmark** control in Opportunity accepts `store_id` or `store_name` plus `rank` or `performance_score`. If at least five Bailey stores match, the best ten temporarily replace the all-Bailey benchmark. The CSV and raw values remain in browser memory and are not uploaded, stored, exported or placed in share URLs.

## Suggested 12-minute walkthrough

### 1. Network — 2 minutes

Show the trans-Tasman store census and switch retailer groups on and off. Explain that major and affiliated networks are mapped at all useful zoom levels, while lower-priority markers appear only when sufficiently zoomed in to avoid crowding. Click a store to show its address, public source, location setting and accepted centre or corridor.

### 2. Places — 2 minutes

Filter to shopping centres, Bailey Nelson absent, and a property group. Open a centre to show exact-place optical tenants, nearby competitors, public ownership or management roles and leasing arrangement. Make clear that a nearby store is not treated as an in-centre tenant unless it shares the accepted canonical place record.

### 3. Opportunity — 3 minutes

Choose Australia and Shopping centres. Add retailer-presence requirements if useful—for example Specsavers present and Oscar Wylee absent. Open a result and start with the Opportunity summary:

- Current rank is within the selected country and location setting.
- Lookalike score compares the place with Bailey Nelson’s mapped footprint using the four visible components.
- Screening completeness is the share of weighted components supported by available public evidence.
- Missing evidence is shown rather than filled with a guess.

### 4. Shortlist and exports — 2 minutes

Bookmark several Opportunity results. Show that the saved-place shortlist persists in this browser but is excluded from share links and network requests. Export:

- Summary CSV for one row per place and its decision evidence.
- Tenant CSV for accepted optical memberships plus researched key co-tenants.
- A printable selected-place brief from the drawer or the context-aware Brief button.

### 5. Property intelligence — 1 minute

Click a property group from a centre. Show its matched public portfolio, Bailey-present properties and Bailey-free portfolio overlap. Explain that portfolio overlap means Bailey Nelson is publicly mapped in another asset controlled by the same group; it is not proof of a private commercial relationship.

### 6. Data Health — 2 minutes

Show store-census health, property-research coverage and demographic health separately. Point out the current ABS vintage, explicit New Zealand demographic limitation, and the remaining centre-research queue. Unknown means the research has not established the fact—not that an owner, manager or tenant does not exist.

## Plain-language definitions

**Lookalike score**  
A 0–100 comparison with Bailey Nelson’s current public footprint. It combines demographic/context similarity, Bailey whitespace, optical-market validation and retail context. It is a screening heuristic—not a sales forecast or probability of success.

**Screening completeness**  
How much of the weighted score is backed by available public evidence. A high score with low completeness deserves more research before comparison.

**Canonical place**  
One stable record for a real centre or street corridor. Stores, property facts and corrections attach to its ID so name changes and aliases do not create duplicate places.

**Portfolio overlap**  
Bailey Nelson is publicly mapped in another property connected to the same owner, manager, operator, leasing controller or external agency. It is relationship intelligence—not a private relationship claim and not part of market attractiveness.

**Exact-place retailer membership**  
The store has accepted evidence linking it to that centre or corridor. Distance alone never establishes membership. A nearby retailer remains labelled nearby.

**ABS demographic context**  
Australia uses ABS SA2 data: 2025 estimated resident population plus selected 2021 Census age and median equivalised weekly household-income measures. Very small-area rates are suppressed where Census perturbation can mislead. Equivalised income is not consumer spending; no verified consumer-spending layer is loaded yet.

**Key co-tenancy profile**  
A curated list of important anchors and Bailey-relevant tenant categories. The fixed top-ten opportunity pilot remains available, and every currently mapped Bailey shopping centre has now received an initial official-source research pass. It is not a complete centre directory.

**Anchor tenant**

A major retailer that helps draw regular visits to a shopping centre, such as a supermarket or department store. It describes the centre environment; it does not prove that an optical store will succeed.

**Co-tenancy**

The mix of other retailers trading in the same centre or corridor. It helps describe the shopping mission and likely customer environment. The full glossary is available from the book icon in the app header.

## Known limitations to state openly

- New Zealand stores and places do not yet have directly comparable official demographic rankings in this public build.
- The broader centre census is materially ahead of property ownership/class research; unknown property facts remain visible.
- All 50 currently mapped Bailey shopping centres have an initial co-tenancy research pass. As of 3 September 2026, 47 have an accepted active anchor profile and 38 have accepted tenants across at least three tracked categories; older or ambiguous evidence remains visibly qualified.
- All 32 Bailey high-street corridors have a checked address-and-optical-membership baseline. Broader non-optical retail-mix profiles are not yet complete and are reported separately rather than implied.
- Independent optical coverage and independent website/social-link enrichment are still incomplete.
- Catchments are indicative straight-line and area-apportioned estimates rather than drive-time trade areas or customer-origin analysis.
- Public centre directories change. Every tenant and relationship should be rechecked before commercial decisions.
- Private Bailey Nelson sales, rent, lease terms and contacts are not stored in this public repository.
