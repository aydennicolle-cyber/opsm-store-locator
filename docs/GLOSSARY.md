# Optical leasing intelligence glossary

These definitions describe how terms are used in the public application. They are written for a leasing and site-selection audience and do not replace legal, valuation or accounting definitions.

- **Anchor tenant:** A major retailer that helps draw regular visits to a shopping centre, such as a supermarket or department store.
- **Bailey whitespace:** Geographic room from existing Bailey Nelson stores, considering nearest-store distance and indicative catchment overlap.
- **Canonical place:** The stable record used to join a real centre or high-street corridor to tenants, property facts and aliases without creating duplicates.
- **Catchment:** A surrounding area used to describe potential customers or nearby competition. Current circular catchments are indicative, not drive-time trade areas.
- **Centre class:** A separate description of a centre's role and scale, such as Regional, Sub-regional or Neighbourhood.
- **Co-tenancy:** The mix of other retailers trading in the same centre or corridor.
- **Competitor validation:** Evidence that optical retailers already trade in a market, indicating demand as well as competition.
- **Confidence:** The strength of the public evidence supporting a fact or mapping.
- **Exact-place membership:** A store-to-place relationship supported by directory, name or address evidence. Proximity alone is insufficient.
- **GLA:** Gross lettable area reported as available for tenant occupation.
- **High-street corridor:** A stable street-and-locality grouping of street-front stores with an indicative 800 metre comparison catchment.
- **Key co-tenancy profile:** A curated selection of decision-relevant tenants, not a complete directory.
- **Leasing controller:** The group that publicly appears to control leasing for a property.
- **Location setting:** Shopping Centre, High Street, Other or Uncertain.
- **Lookalike score:** A public-evidence screening comparison with Bailey Nelson's mapped footprint, not a success probability.
- **Mapped optical tenant:** An optical storefront with an accepted canonical place relationship.
- **Portfolio overlap:** Evidence that Bailey Nelson trades in another property linked to the same public property group or agency; not proof of a private relationship.
- **Screening completeness:** The proportion of expected inputs available for a screening result.
- **Source freshness:** How recently the public source was checked against its refresh policy.
- **Trade area:** The market from which a centre or store draws customers; distinct from an indicative circular catchment.
